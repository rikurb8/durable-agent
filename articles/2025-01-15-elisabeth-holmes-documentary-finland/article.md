## Realistic routes to watch it in Finland

Since no Finland date exists yet, here is how to actually find it:

1. **Watch Finnish cinema listings as they update.** The main chain is **Finnkino** (17 cinemas, 12 locations). Check its "all films" page — if a Finnish distributor picks it up, it will appear there. (https://www.finnkino.fi/en/films/all-films)
2. **Check JustWatch for Finland** and set an alert. JustWatch tracks availability across Finnish streaming services and cinemas and lets you "notify me." (https://www.justwatch.com/fi/ — search the title)
3. **Streaming is likely later and region-dependent.** A24's films typically go to streaming after the theatrical window. One trade report speculates a **streaming release around January 2027, "most likely on HBO Max"** — but that is the *Australia/NZ* timeline and is explicitly an *expectation*, not a confirmed date, and may differ for Finland. (RUSSH, https://www.russh.com/elizabeth-holmes-nathan-fielder-documentary/)
4. **Note on A24's Finnish distribution:** A24 distributes directly in the US; international rights are handled by local distributors per territory. That is why a Finland date can lag and appear only when a Finnish/Nordic distributor announces it. (List of A24 films, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_A24_films)
